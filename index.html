    History of Cast

    BL was formed in Berkeley in 1995 after the legendary weekly shadow cast Indecent Exposure called it quits. BL was created to fill that major hole in the Berkeley community and continue Rocky Horror at the UC.

    Unfortunately, the original UC Theatre closed in 2001, but BL continued to perform in the meantime, calling the New Parkway (Oakland), Camera 3/3Below (San Jose), and the Albany Twin our homes for many years. They have all since closed. Make of that what you will.

    We have seasonally called the Phoenix Theatre in Petaluma our home away from home after resident cast Absolute Pleasure dissolved.
    Over the years we have taken our show all over the Bay Area and greater Northern California.

    But our home and heart were always in Berkeley, and in 2016, when the UC Theatre reopened as a live entertainment venue, we were delighted to bring the show back home. You can catch us there for what have become annual traditions around Valentine's Day and Halloween.

    What Is a Shadow Cast?

    Shadow casting has been a Rocky Horror tradition since the late '70s. It is the natural marriage of theater and film.

    While it is not exclusive to Rocky Horror, Rocky takes a whole lot of credit for the origin and made it a staple of screenings. We stand in front of the screen and act out the entire movie, in full costume, with props. We try our best, or our worst, to be Screen Accurate.
